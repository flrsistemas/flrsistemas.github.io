🚀Getting started3 questions

How do I create my account?

On the app's first screen, tap "Cadastre-se" (Sign up), enter your name, e-mail and a password of at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. You get an e-mail to confirm the sign-up; then just sign in.

I did not get the confirmation e-mail

Wait a few minutes and check your spam or junk folder. The e-mail comes from "Supabase Auth", the service that handles sign-in. If it still does not arrive, write to us with the e-mail you used.

How do I install it on my phone?

On Android (Chrome), open the app, tap the ⋮ menu and "Install app" or "Add to home screen".

On iPhone (Safari), tap Share and "Add to Home Screen".

🛒Lists and items5 questions

How do I create a list?

On the "Minhas listas" (My lists) screen, type a name, like "Monthly groceries", and tap "Criar" (Create).

How do I add, edit and check items?

Inside the list, type the name in "Adicionar item…" (Add item…), pick the category (the app suggests one) and, if you like, enter quantity and price. Tap "Adicionar" (Add).

To mark an item as bought, tap its checkbox. To edit, tap the name. To remove it, tap the ✕.

How do I set item prices?

Use the "Preço (R$)" field when adding the item, or tap the item to edit it. The total shows inside the list and on its card. The app does not multiply by quantity: enter the price for the whole item.

How do I clear the items already bought?

In the list, tap "Limpar comprados" (Clear bought), next to the "Comprados" heading. This removes only the checked items, for every member.

How do I delete a list?

Open the list, tap the people icon (👥) and "Excluir lista" (Delete list). This deletes the list and all its items for every member and cannot be undone. Only the owner sees this button.

🤝Sharing4 questions

How do I share a list?

Open the list, tap the people icon (👥), enter the e-mail of who will join and tap "Compartilhar" (Share). Only the list owner can share it.

It says "Nenhum usuário cadastrado com este e-mail"

That means no account exists with that e-mail. The person needs to create an account in the app first, with that same e-mail. Then share again.

How do I remove someone from the list?

Open the list, tap 👥 and "Remover" (Remove) next to the name. Only the owner can remove members.

How do I leave a list that was shared with me?

Open the list, tap 👥 and "Sair da lista" (Leave list). This removes only you; the list stays for the others.

I forgot my password

The app does not have password recovery on the sign-in screen yet. Write to us from the e-mail you registered with and we will help you get back in.

How do I delete my account and my data?

Write to us from the registered e-mail asking for deletion. See the privacy policy to know what is deleted.

Does it work offline?

You can view the last saved version of your lists. To add, check or remove items you need to be online.
